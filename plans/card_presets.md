# Card presets build sheet

Passive cards are left out on purpose. They work without being equipped:
- 57 cards marked Passive (Griffy, wiki).
- Every Mining, Chopping, Fishing, Catching, Trapping and Worship card. Your account totals are all past 300, which makes them passive (Rift Skill Mastery, wiki). Griffy: once skill cards are passive you don't need separate skill sets.

Preset numbers are the same on everyone: 1 Fighting AFK, 2 Skilling, 3 Drop rate, 4 Active EXP, 5 Spelunking, 6 Crystal hunt, 7 Cooking or Lab.
Slots go left to right, top row 1-4, bottom row 5-8. "(doubled)" means that slot is doubled by your lab chip.
Card set is picked separately from the preset. Set it when you switch.

Missing: you own no drop rate multi card yet (Coralcave Guardian, Mantaray, Ancientfish). Pulse: push one fast, then double it. When you get one, it replaces the weakest card in presets 3 and 6.
