# Sneaking: charm rolls / symbol rolls (Zack 2026-11-21, from his own lookup)
- Symbol Rolls (75/day): once unlocked via Strange Symbols, a charm roll can turn into a consumable Symbol.
- Conversion chance starts at 25% and drops by 0.33% per roll, reaching 0% after 75 rolls.
- Toolbox shows "Daily charm rolls used x / 120" on the Sneaking page.
