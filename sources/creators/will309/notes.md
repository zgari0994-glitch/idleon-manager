## Recurring fix list (appears in nearly every review — his standing advice)
- **Prayers**: run the combat set — Skilled Dimwit, Balance of Pain, Beefy for Real ⚠, Shiny Snitch (+ Royal Sampler while prints are under 90%); Rucksack only during prints/stamp work. Wrong or passive prayers are the #1 silent loss he finds.
- **Cards**: stop running passive cards for fighting; fix card sets; push cards toward six-star going into W7. Mob prints belong ONLY on the Vman and Wind Walker — reprint everything else.
- **Obols**: roll for pure DROP RATE, not luck.
- **Food**: stop using regular food; farm golden food (≥1M of it).
- **Gear floor**: everyone gets full tools + traps + skulls + splicers, upgraded; dungeon keychains, Emperor Opal rings, Divine scarves; don't craft mid-game gear — jump to Troll/Divine, then Prehistoric/Godshard.
- **Statues**: onyx/zenith them and push to a baseline (150 early, 300 mid, 350 late) — and do the Monolith quest on EVERY character.
- **Spend what's sitting idle**: anvil points/materials and Post Office boxes are maxed/spent, never hoarded; PO boxes should be maxed before asking about endgame content.
- **Alchemy**: bubbles to high efficiency on the early pages (70% → 80% → 95% by stage), vials worked on, Kilroy faster-respawn toward 40, weekly Kilroy.
- **Stamps**: stage targets recur — 12k entering W6, 15k, then 18k.
- **Equinox**: max the symbols FIRST; never put points in Food Loss.
- **Worship/TD**: spend worship charge; push Tower Defense (targets ~180–200).
- **Divinity**: fix stances; God rank floor rises by stage (12 → 15 → 20 in a good AFK week).
- **DK farm**: farm Glounko ⚠ to ~100k kills on the Divine Knight.
- **Cooking**: refocus with ladles ("one-ladle wonder" if behind).
- **Refinery**: stop auto-refining red salts; use the salt-ratio guide.
- **The meta-rule**: fix silent foundational bonuses BEFORE chasing kills/hour or new-world content — an old account is not the same as a long-played one.
